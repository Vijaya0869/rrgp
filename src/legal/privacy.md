This notice explains what information R&R Grandeur Properties ("we", "us") collects through rrgp.io and how we use it.

## What we collect

- **Information you send us.** When you submit an opportunity or join the marketplace list, we receive what you enter: your name, email address, phone number, property details, asking price and any message.
- **Basic technical data.** Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard request data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other websites.

## How we use it

We use the information you send to review your opportunity, respond to you, and, if you asked, tell you when the marketplace opens. We do not sell or rent personal information.

## Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that run this website for us: our website host, the service that delivers form submissions to our inbox, and the provider of the site's fonts. They may process data only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep submissions for as long as needed to evaluate and follow up on an opportunity, or until you ask us to delete them, unless we must keep them longer for legal or record-keeping reasons.

## Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you have sent us, or to remove you from the marketplace list, by emailing [deals@rrgp.io](mailto:deals@rrgp.io).

## Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The date above shows when it last changed.
