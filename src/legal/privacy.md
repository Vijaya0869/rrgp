R&R Grandeur Properties and its affiliated companies, JR Grand Homes and JR Elite Rentals ("R&R", "we", "us" or "our"), respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit rrgp.io (the "Site"), how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site or submit information through it.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you submit a deal or opportunity, join the marketplace waiting list, or contact us, we collect what you provide. This may include:

- your name, email address and phone number;
- your company or role;
- property details, such as location, asking price, condition and photographs; and
- any other information you include in your message.

**Information collected automatically.** When you visit the Site, our hosting and content-delivery providers automatically record standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed and the date and time of your visit. This information is used to operate and secure the Site.

**Information from others.** We may receive information about you from brokers, wholesalers, lenders, partners or other people who refer an opportunity to us.

## Cookies and tracking

The Site does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or third-party tracking pixels, and we do not track you across other websites. Our service providers may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies to deliver the Site securely, for example to block malicious traffic.

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" setting. Because we do not track visitors across websites, the Site does not change its behavior in response to that signal.

## How we use your information

We use personal information to:

- review, evaluate and respond to opportunities you submit;
- reply to your questions and communicate with you;
- notify you when the R&R Marketplace opens, if you asked us to;
- operate, maintain, secure and improve the Site;
- detect and prevent fraud, spam and abuse; and
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## How we share your information

We share personal information only as described below:

- **Affiliated companies.** With JR Grand Homes, JR Elite Rentals and other companies under common ownership with R&R, to evaluate and carry out an opportunity.
- **Service providers.** With vendors that help us run the Site and our business, including our website host and content-delivery network, our form-handling provider, the provider of the Site's web fonts and our email provider. They may use the information only to provide their services to us.
- **Professional advisers and partners.** With our attorneys, accountants, lenders and capital partners where reasonably necessary to evaluate or complete a transaction you have proposed.
- **Legal reasons.** When we believe disclosure is required by law, subpoena or court order, or is needed to protect the rights, property or safety of R&R, our users or others.
- **Business transfers.** In connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, sale of assets or similar transaction, in which case personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
- **With your consent.** In any other case where you have asked or agreed to share it.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal information for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, such as evaluating and following up on an opportunity, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law, for example for tax, accounting or record-keeping purposes. When we no longer need it, we delete it or make it anonymous.

## How we protect it

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections to the Site. No method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and rights

You may ask us to:

- tell you what personal information we hold about you;
- correct information that is inaccurate;
- delete your information; or
- stop sending you marketplace updates.

Each email update includes a way to unsubscribe. To make any other request, email [deals@rrgp.io](mailto:deals@rrgp.io). We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy laws, and you will not be treated differently for exercising them.

## Children's privacy

The Site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Links to other websites

The Site may link to websites that we do not operate, such as JR Capital. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

## Visitors outside the United States

The Site is operated in the United States and is intended for visitors in the United States. If you visit from elsewhere, your information will be processed in the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your country.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
