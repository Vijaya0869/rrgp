This Privacy Policy explains how R&R Grandeur Properties and its affiliated companies, JR Grand Homes and JR Elite Rentals ("R&R," "we," "us" or "our"), collect, use, share and protect personal information in connection with the website at rrgp.io (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

## 1. Information we collect

### Information you provide

When you submit a deal or opportunity, join the marketplace waiting list or contact us, we collect what you give us. This may include:

- your name, email address and phone number;
- your company or role;
- property details, such as location, asking price, condition and photographs; and
- anything else you include in your message.

### Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, our hosting and content-delivery provider records standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed and the date and time of your visit. This information is used to operate and secure the Site.

### Information from others

We may receive information about you from brokers, wholesalers, lenders, partners or other people who refer an opportunity to us.

## 2. How we use information

We use personal information:

- to review, evaluate and respond to opportunities you submit;
- to answer your questions and communicate with you;
- to tell you when the R&R Marketplace opens, if you asked us to;
- to operate, maintain, secure and improve the Site;
- to detect and prevent fraud, spam and abuse; and
- to comply with legal obligations and enforce our [Terms of Use](/terms/).

## 3. How we share information

**We do not sell or rent your personal information,** and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may share it only:

- with JR Grand Homes, JR Elite Rentals and other companies under common ownership with R&R, to evaluate and carry out an opportunity;
- with service providers that help us run the Site and our business, who may use it only to provide their services to us;
- with attorneys, accountants, lenders and capital partners, where reasonably needed to evaluate or complete a transaction you have proposed;
- when we believe disclosure is required by law, subpoena or court order, or is needed to protect the rights, property or safety of R&R or others;
- in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, sale of assets or similar transaction; and
- in any other case where you have asked or agreed to share it.

## 4. Forms, hosting and third-party services

The Site is hosted and delivered through Cloudflare, which processes technical information to deliver the Site and protect it from malicious traffic. The Site's web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google and shares your IP address with it when a page loads. Information you send through our forms is delivered to us by a form-handling provider, and our email provider handles the messages we exchange with you. Each provider handles information under its own privacy policy.

## 5. Cookies and tracking

The Site does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or third-party tracking pixels, and we do not track you across other websites. Our service providers may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies to deliver the Site securely, for example to block malicious traffic.

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" setting. Because we do not track visitors across websites, the Site does not change its behavior in response to that signal.

## 6. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, such as evaluating and following up on an opportunity, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law, for example for tax, accounting or record-keeping purposes. When we no longer need it, we delete it or make it anonymous.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections to the Site. No method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to:

- tell you what personal information we hold about you;
- correct information that is inaccurate;
- delete your information; or
- stop sending you marketplace updates.

Each email update includes a way to unsubscribe. To make any other request, email [deals@rrgp.io](mailto:deals@rrgp.io). We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy laws, and you will not be treated differently for exercising them.

## 9. Children's privacy

The Site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Other websites and visitors outside the United States

The Site may link to websites we do not operate, such as JR Capital. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

The Site is operated in the United States and is intended for visitors in the United States. If you visit from elsewhere, your information will be processed in the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your country.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
